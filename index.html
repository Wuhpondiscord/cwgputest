Research notes

The test compares the same workload with and without shader instrumentation, reports validation correctness and measures buffer readback. It does not reveal driver counters, physical VRAM usage, or arbitrary GPU execution state.

Next experiments: tensor snapshotting, WGSL source transforms, dispatch-level checkpointing, and memory-pooling benchmarks.
