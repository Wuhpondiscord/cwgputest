Measurement interpretation

Compilation is measured separately from steady-state trials. CPU wall times include scheduling and buffer readback. GPU timestamps are recorded with raw values for diagnosis, but are not automatically assumed trustworthy. Atomic counters measure shader invocations, not native hardware activity.

Potential next steps: matrix multiplication, buffer reuse/pooling, checkpoint serialization, subgroup kernels, and tensor snapshots.
